One-sentence: a radio that needs explaining gets a card — selected state is a full white 1px border, not a lime one.

```jsx
<RadioCard checked name="tipo" radioSide="left" title="Projeto" description="Tem início e conclusão" />
<RadioCard name="tipo" radioSide="left" title="Rotina" description="Repete dentro de uma área" />
```

Lay them out in a grid of 2–3 equal columns.
