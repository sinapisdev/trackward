Referência visual

Design system, para olhar

As páginas que vieram do export, guardadas para consulta. Elas não fazem parte do app: desenham com os mesmos tokens de styles.css, e é só.

Precisam de servidor, porque montam os componentes buscando as fontes de components/. Na raiz do projeto: npm run ds:referencia, e abra http://localhost:4321/referencia/. Abrir por file:// não funciona, o navegador barra a leitura dos arquivos vizinhos.

Fundamentos

  • Logo lockupsTwo done checkpoints and one open — never redraw or re-space it
  • VoicePortuguese, second person, one next step at a time
  • White alphasHairlines, hovers and glass are white at 4–20%
  • Brand limeOne accent, used for the single forward action
  • Ink rampFive steps of text on dark — body copy sits at --ink-1
  • Situação coloursAtrasado, vence em breve, em dia — colour never travels alone
  • Dark surfacesNear-black page up to the folder-card body
  • ElevationShadows are deep and soft; borders are 1px inner light
  • Glass & focus18px backdrop blur on folders; lime 2px focus ring
  • IconographyLucide, 1.75 stroke — the only glyph set
  • Motion120ms hovers, 280ms reveals, one easing curve
  • Corner radiiPills for controls, 10px fields, 18px folders
  • Layout frameFixed chrome: 66px bar, 296px rail, 456px panel, 34px gutters
  • Spacing scale4px base; 12 / 16 / 24 carry most layouts
  • Body, UI & meta15px body, 14px controls, 13px meta
  • Display & title44 / 40px, bold, tight tracking — greetings and page titles
  • HeadingsSection, panel and card headings
  • Kickers & metricsUppercase type labels and the big progress figure
  • Figtree weightsSubstitution for the unshipped brand face — 400/500/600/700

Componentes, estado por estado

  • Agenda — week gridDeadlines band, events, and hatched external busy time
  • Core — buttons, chips, avatarsLime pill for the forward move; outline and chip for everything else
  • Forms — fields and choicesLime focus ring, 46px controls, white selection states
  • Navigation — top bar, tabs, railsRoutes live in the top bar; rails only hold areas and channels
  • Status — trilha, progress, situaçõesThe trilha plus the fixed vocabulary of work states
  • Surfaces — folders, tables, panelsGlass folder cards and hairline tables
  • Work — tasks, conversa, propostas da IATask rows, messages and the AI review hand-off
